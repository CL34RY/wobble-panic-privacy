Wobble Panic Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Summary

Wobble Panic collects no personal information itself: there is no account, no sign-up and no analytics, and your progress stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your device goes to Google, when you choose to watch an ad, and to Apple, if you use Game Center. This policy explains both. "We" means the developer of Wobble Panic.

Information kept on your device

The game saves a few things on your phone so it remembers you between games. They are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your best height, number of runs, coins, helpers bought and any active coin boost
  • Your daily challenge results for today
  • Your settings (sound on or off)
  • The player names you typed in party mode

We have no servers and cannot see this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Ads in Wobble Panic are always your choice. The game never shows an ad on its own: you can tap to watch one after a run to double your coins, or in the Shop for a 30-minute coin boost.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad loads or plays, Google may collect:

  • Your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location (country or region)
  • Device information, such as the phone model, operating system and language
  • Information about the ads you see and whether you watch them through

We only request non-personalized ads. That means ads are not chosen based on your interests or your activity in other apps, and the game never asks for permission to track you across apps.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ad is loaded. You can play the whole game without ever watching an ad.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your daily challenge scores are sent to Apple's daily leaderboard. Other players can see your Game Center nickname and score there. Game Center is optional, and you can sign out in your iPhone's Settings.

We receive no information from Game Center beyond the leaderboard itself. Apple handles Game Center data under its own privacy policy.

Sharing photos and clips

When you tap Share after a run, the game makes a picture or video clip of your collapse on your phone and opens the iPhone's share menu. You choose where it goes, such as Messages or TikTok. Nothing is uploaded by us, and nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Children

Wobble Panic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game itself collects none from anyone. If you believe a child's information has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Ads: you never have to watch one. If you were asked for ad consent, you can change your answer at any time from the Privacy choices option in the game.
  • Your saved progress: deleting the app deletes everything it saved on your phone.
  • Game Center: you can sign out, or reset your Game Center data, in your iPhone's Settings.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information. We do not have any to sell.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Because we hold none, requests about ad data are best made to Google, and about Game Center to Apple. You can still contact us with any question.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
